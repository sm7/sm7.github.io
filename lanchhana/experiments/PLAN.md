# Dynasty places: sourcing rules and plan

## Rules (standing)
1. A scene exists only if a primary text gives an honest picture of that place. No source, no scene; say so on the card.
2. Cite the original work (author, edition, page or verse), linked to an original scan or a publisher/archive copy (archive.org, Project Gutenberg, DHARMA, ASI, SII/EI volumes). Never cite or link Wikipedia or Wikisource. They may be used only to find the original reference.
3. Each scene text is split into Attested (the primary text), Second source, Conjecture (what the painter had to invent).
4. Open-access, public-domain scans only.
5. Paint in the site's current style (realistic digital painting, natural light, period dress: unstitched cloth, no turbans or kurtas). Show images to the user and wire only after approval.

## State (2026-10-06)
43 dynasty cards. 16 have scenes, all citing primary texts (Megasthenes in Strabo, Faxian, Xuanzang, Periplus, Rājataraṅgiṇī, Paes, Sulaymān, SII/EI inscriptions). 27 have none.

## Candidate primary texts for dynasties still without scenes (not yet read; to verify)
- Kṣatrapa: Junagadh rock inscription of Rudradāman (EI 8 no. 6); Periplus 41–49 (Barygaza market: imports and exports listed).
- Śātavāhana: Periplus 51–52 (Paithana, Tagara, Kalliena); Nāsik cave inscriptions (EI 8).
- Cera / Pāṇḍya: Periplus 53–56 (Muziris, Nelcynda); Sangam poems (Paṭṭiṉappālai, Maduraikkāñci).
- Pratihāra: Sulaymān and al-Masʿūdī on Kanauj (Elliot and Dowson, vol. 1).
- Pāla: Pāla copper-plate grants; Nālandā grants.
- Western Gaṅga: Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa inscriptions (EC vol. 2).
- Kākatīya: Motupalli abhayaśāsana (Gaṇapatideva, 1244); Marco Polo on Mutfili.
- Mewar: Chittor and Kumbhalgarh praśastis; Rāṇakpur inscription.
- Ahom: Buranjis (Bhuyan trans.).
- Vijayanagara (more): Nuniz, Abdur Razzaq (Sewell, A Forgotten Empire).
- Others (Paramāra, Chandela, Sena, Gahaḍavāla, Chauhan, Yādava, Kalachuri, Śāhi, Gauḍa, Maukhari, Vākāṭaka-era, Viṣṇukuṇḍin, Śarabhapurīya, Pāṇḍuvaṁśī, Eastern Gaṅga, Tripura, Maratha, Sikh, Travancore, Jaipur): to be searched, praśasti and chronicle first.

## Notes on reading scans
archive.org full text (`/download/<id>/<id>_djvu.txt`) is usable but OCR is noisy; take quotations only from a verified page image or a clean edition. Periplus, Schoff 1912: archive.org id `periplusoferythr00schouoft`; §§38–56 (Barygaza to Nelcynda) located.
