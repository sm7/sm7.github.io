# Chera: Muziris harbour, c. 60 CE

One scene. Source read verbatim: Periplus of the Erythraean Sea §§53–56 (c. 40–70 CE), tr. Schoff 1912, p. 44–45 (archive.org periplusoferythr00scho).

Attested: Muziris, in the kingdom of Cerobothra (Chera), "abounds in ships sent there with cargoes from Arabia, and by the Greeks"; on a river, 500 stadia from Tyndis, and 20 stadia up the river from the shore; the river is full of shoals and channels not clear, so ships anchor at the roadstead of Bacare and cargo is brought down; "large ships" sent for pepper and malabathrum. Imports: coin, topaz, thin clothing, figured linens, antimony, coral, crude glass, copper, tin, lead, wine, realgar, orpiment, wheat for sailors. Exports: pepper (from Cottonara), fine pearls, ivory, silk cloth, spikenard, malabathrum, transparent stones, diamonds, sapphires, tortoise-shell. The kings live inland.
Not in the source: any description of the town's buildings or the king's court, so the town is conjecture. No temple or street scene is possible.
Conjecture: thatched warehouses, ship and boat forms, dress, crowd.
