# Scenes plan: all dynasties

Rule: a dynasty gets scenes only if a primary source describes its capital, or its great temple/monument, in the era of its most renowned king, closely enough to paint without inventing. Tiers below are my first guesses from memory, NOT yet checked against the texts. A tier is confirmed only after the source is read.

Done: maurya (Candragupta; Strabo/Diodorus/Arthaśāstra), vijayanagara (Kṛṣṇadevarāya; Paes, Razzaq, Nuniz via Sewell).

## Tier A: strong eyewitness or contemporary description (do first)
pushyabhuti (Harṣa; Xuanzang, Bāṇa) · gupta (Faxian) · kushan (Faxian/Xuanzang on Kanishka's stūpa) · ahom (Talish 1662, Buranji) · sikh (Ranjit Singh; European visitors) · maratha (Śivājī; Raigad; Fryer, Sabhāsad) · mysore (Srirangapatna) · pandya (Maduraikkāñci) · karkota (Kalhaṇa on Lalitāditya) · chera (Early Cheras; Muziris in Periplus, Pliny, Sangam)

## Per dynasty
1. Read the source (verbatim text via archive.org full text in Chrome, or another open host). 2. Write briefs.md with Attested / Other sources / Conjecture. 3. Three images (city, street, temple or the nearest honest equivalents), naturalistic style. 4. Review against briefs, send to the user. 5. After approval: scenes/<id>/scenes.json and tools/add_scenes.py.
